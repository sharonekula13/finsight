NOVATECH CORPORATION — FORM 10-K (FISCAL YEAR 2024) — ILLUSTRATIVE SAMPLE

Item 1. Business.
NovaTech Corporation is a fictional enterprise software company used here as
sample data. NovaTech designs cloud infrastructure software, data analytics
platforms, and developer tooling. The company operates three reportable
segments: Cloud Platform, Data Analytics, and Developer Tools.

Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis.
NovaTech reported total revenue of 4.2 billion dollars in fiscal 2024, an
increase of 18 percent year over year, driven primarily by growth in the Cloud
Platform segment. The Cloud Platform segment was the company's largest business
segment, representing 62 percent of total revenue in fiscal 2024. The Data
Analytics segment contributed 26 percent of revenue, and the Developer Tools
segment contributed the remaining 12 percent. Research and development expense
was 610 million dollars in fiscal 2024, or approximately 15 percent of revenue.
Operating margin for fiscal 2024 was 19 percent, up from 16 percent in the prior
year, reflecting improved operating leverage in the Cloud Platform segment.

Item 1A. Risk Factors.
NovaTech is exposed to customer concentration risk. In fiscal 2024, a single
customer accounted for 22 percent of total revenue, and the loss of that
customer would materially harm the company's results of operations. NovaTech
also faces intense competition, rapid technological change, and risks related
to data security and privacy regulation across the jurisdictions in which it
operates.
